# Strategic Advocacy Lifecycle

> Status: foundation working model; not a declaration that broader governance-layer lifecycle migrations are complete.

The manual uses a practical advocacy lifecycle to organise work while preserving human decision authority.

## 1. Frame

Define the issue, desired change, scope, constraints, decision horizon, and success conditions.

## 2. Understand

Build an evidence-aware view of the environment, system, stakeholders, power, incentives, narratives, risks, and uncertainty.

## 3. Strategise

Choose outcomes, pathways of influence, audiences, positioning, theory of change, resource allocation, and strategic hypotheses.

## 4. Design

Translate strategy into engagement plans, messages, coalitions, interventions, sequencing, responsibilities, safeguards, and measures.

## 5. Act

Conduct authorised advocacy activities while recording material decisions, evidence, outputs, and deviations.

## 6. Observe

Monitor signals, outcomes, stakeholder responses, risks, assumptions, and unintended effects.

## 7. Learn and adapt

Assess evidence against strategic hypotheses, update confidence, revise plans where authorised, and preserve lessons for reuse.

## 8. Close or renew

Record outcomes, unresolved issues, retained knowledge, and the human decision to close, continue, or redesign the advocacy effort.
