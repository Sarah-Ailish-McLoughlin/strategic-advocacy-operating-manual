# Scope

## In scope

This manual covers the operating discipline required to design, conduct, review, and improve strategic advocacy, including:

- objective and outcome definition;
- context, system, stakeholder, power, and influence analysis;
- evidence and assumptions;
- strategy and theory of change;
- audience and engagement planning;
- message and narrative development;
- tactics, sequencing, and coordination;
- decision records and governance;
- monitoring, evaluation, learning, and adaptation;
- reusable playbooks, methods, templates, and examples.

## Out of scope at foundation stage

The repository does not claim to provide jurisdiction-specific legal advice, autonomous decision authority, or a complete automated advocacy system. Machine execution, observability, and machine-evolvable governance are later maturity targets and require explicit controls before adoption.
