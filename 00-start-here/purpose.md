# Purpose

The Strategic Advocacy Operating Manual exists to make strategic advocacy more deliberate, evidence-aware, reusable, governable, and capable of progressive machine assistance.

It provides a common operating frame for moving from an advocacy objective to a governed programme of analysis, strategy, engagement, action, learning, and adaptation.

The manual is intended to support practitioners and organisations without assuming that advocacy is a single campaign technique. It treats advocacy as an operating capability whose decisions, evidence, methods, outputs, and learning can be made explicit.

## Foundation objectives

- provide clear human-readable guidance;
- define reusable strategic advocacy concepts and practices;
- preserve evidence and decision provenance;
- introduce structured machine-readable representations;
- enable later validation and authorised automation without prematurely granting machine authority.
