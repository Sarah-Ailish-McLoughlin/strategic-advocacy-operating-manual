# Strategy Development Playbook

## Outcome

Produce a reviewable advocacy strategy whose objective, assumptions, evidence, actions, risks and decision rights are explicit.

## Workflow

1. **Frame** — establish the problem, context, scope and evidence base.
2. **Define** — state the advocacy objective and observable success conditions.
3. **Map** — identify authority, stakeholders, power, affected communities and relationships.
4. **Model** — develop a theory of change and expose material assumptions.
5. **Design** — select narrative, engagement and tactics that fit the objective and evidence.
6. **Safeguard** — assess risks, representation, privacy, legal/compliance and unintended effects.
7. **Measure** — define indicators and learning signals before execution.
8. **Review** — obtain required human approval and record the decision.
9. **Execute** — conduct only authorised activity.
10. **Learn** — update evidence and recommend adaptation based on observed results.

## Required artefacts

At foundation maturity, use:

- `templates/advocacy-strategy.md`
- `methods/stakeholder-power-mapping.md`
- `methods/theory-of-change.md`
- `templates/evidence-record.yaml`

## Authority boundary

AI or automation may assist with research synthesis, drafting, consistency checks and proposed adaptations. It must not independently approve a strategy, determine canonical policy, impersonate stakeholders, or undertake consequential external engagement without the relevant human authority.
